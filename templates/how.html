About

Saved TikToks, turned into a trip.

You keep saving travel TikToks and never look at them again. Travel Saver reads each one for you and pulls out the places worth remembering, sorted into the city you're planning.

1

Create a city trip

One trip per city — Tokyo and Kyoto are separate. Pick the country, then the city.

2

Paste TikToks

Videos, photo carousels and short links. We read the caption, transcribe the audio, and OCR the on-screen text.

3

Review what we found

Every place shows where it was mentioned and how sure we are. You stay in charge of what makes the trip.

Nothing is invented. A place only appears if the creator actually named, visited or recommended it in the caption, audio or on-screen text. Street names on a map screenshot, background signage and generic categories like "a rooftop café" are left out — or flagged for you under Needs your review.

What the labels mean

Hover any label in the app for the same explanation

Place types

one per place

A fixed set, so "cafe", "coffee shop" and "café" never become three different labels. Anything the AI can't fit gets Other.

Tags

up to four per video or place

Who a place is for and when to go. These are what you'll filter by when planning.

Confidence

per place

How sure we are that this is a real, specific place the creator recommends.

Usefulness

per video

How much a TikTok gives you to actually plan with.

Sources

per place

Green means the place was mentioned in that source. A place found in all three is very unlikely to be a mistake.

Notes

per video

Useful things that aren't a pin on a map — dishes to try, etiquette, when to go.
